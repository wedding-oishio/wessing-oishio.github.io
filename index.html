The Journey

Our Story

What started as a chance meeting in a quiet bookstore in London evolved into a journey across three continents and countless memories. Julian’s quiet strength and Evelyn’s vibrant spirit found a perfect harmony that neither knew they were searching for.

From rainy strolls along the Seine to starry nights in the Tuscan countryside, our love has grown in the quiet moments of everyday life. Today, we stand ready to write our next chapter, surrounded by the people who mean the most.
